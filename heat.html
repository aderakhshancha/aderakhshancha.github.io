Heat prediction

The Miami Heat are looking to have a competitive 2026–27 NBA season as they aim to make another deep playoff run in the Eastern Conference. Led by Bam Adebayo and Tyler Herro, the Heat have strong scoring, defensive ability, and valuable experience. Their biggest strengths are their physical defense, teamwork, and ability to perform under pressure. However, inconsistent shooting and injuries could create challenges throughout the season. I predict Miami will finish with approximately 43–47 wins, earn a playoff spot, and reach the Eastern Conference Semifinals. If Adebayo and Herro stay healthy and the supporting cast performs consistently, the Heat could surprise several teams and become a dangerous playoff opponent.
