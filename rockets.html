Rockets prediction

I predict the Houston Rockets will have a very successful 2026–27 NBA season, finishing with around a 51–31 record and earning the 4th seed in the Western Conference. With Kevin Durant leading the team in scoring, Alperen Şengün continuing to develop into an elite center, and Amen Thompson taking a major leap in his overall game, Houston has the potential to become one of the most dangerous teams in the NBA. I expect Fred VanVleet's return to provide valuable leadership and improve the team's offense, while Jabari Smith Jr. and Tari Eason will play important roles in rebounding, defense, and outside shooting. The Rockets' biggest strengths will be their size, athleticism, defensive intensity, and depth, but their biggest challenges will be staying healthy, improving their three-point shooting, and executing in close games. I believe Houston will finish in the top five of the Western Conference, win its first-round playoff series, and make it to the Western Conference semifinals. If Amen Thompson becomes a consistent scorer and Şengün continues improving, I wouldn't be surprised if the Rockets make an even deeper playoff run. Overall, I believe the Rockets have a legitimate chance to compete for a championship and could surprise a lot of people this season.
