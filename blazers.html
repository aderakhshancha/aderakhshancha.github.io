The Portland Trail Blazers are a professional basketball team based in Portland, Oregon, and compete in the NBA's Western Conference as part of the Northwest Division. Founded in 1970, the Trail Blazers have a passionate fan base and a long history in professional basketball. Their biggest achievement came in 1977, when they won their first and only NBA championship, led by legendary players such as Bill Walton. Throughout their history, the team has also featured stars like Clyde Drexler and Damian Lillard. Portland is known for its loyal supporters, exciting basketball atmosphere, and strong connection with its community. In the 2026–27 NBA season, Portland has an exciting new-look roster featuring Damian Lillard, Ja Morant, Deni Avdija, Scoot Henderson, and Donovan Clingan. Lillard brings veteran leadership and three-point shooting, while Morant adds speed, athleticism, and explosive scoring. Avdija provides versatility and all-around offensive ability, and Clingan gives the team size, rebounding, and interior defense. Head coach Micah Nori will be responsible for bringing all these talents together and building a successful team. Portland's goal is to become more competitive in the Western Conference and make another playoff run. The Trail Blazers play their home games at the Moda Center and are known by their fans as Rip City. Overall, Portland is a team with a rich history, exciting young talent, experienced stars, and plenty of potential for the future.
