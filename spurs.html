Spurs prediction

Why I believe in them San Antonio is my pick to finish with the best record in the West. They have something that very few teams possess: a generational defensive player surrounded by young players who can grow together. Victor Wembanyama is the biggest reason. He changes the geometry of the basketball court. Teams have to think twice before attacking the basket, and that affects opponents even when he isn't recording a block. The supporting cast is also extremely exciting: De'Aaron Fox gives them elite speed and transition offense. Stephon Castle provides physical defense and playmaking. Dylan Harper gives them another potential high-level creator. Devin Vassell supplies shooting and spacing. Tobias Harris adds veteran experience. What I expect I expect Wembanyama to have an MVP-caliber season. My rough projection is 27–29 points, 11–12 rebounds and 3–4 blocks per game. The most important development will be the chemistry between Fox, Castle and Harper. There are three guards who want the ball, so managing their roles will be important. Biggest weakness: Perimeter shooting consistency and the challenge of fitting multiple ball-dominant guards together. Playoff prediction: Western Conference Finals at minimum. My verdict: San Antonio wins the West in the regular season. I genuinely believe this team could win the championship.
