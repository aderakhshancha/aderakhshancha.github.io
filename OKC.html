Thunder prediction

Why they could finish first I still consider Oklahoma City one of the two best teams in the West. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is an incredible offensive engine. He can score from the midrange, attack the basket, draw fouls and create opportunities for teammates. The key players: Jalen Williams needs to return to his best level after his injury problems. Chet Holmgren is essential for their interior defense. Shai continues to provide superstar-level consistency. Their younger players give them considerable depth. The concern is that they lost important rotation players, including Lu Dort, Isaiah Joe and Aaron Wiggins. That matters because their previous success was built on having excellent players beyond their starting five. What I expect Shai will remain an MVP candidate, and I expect OKC to finish with one of the best defensive records in basketball. My biggest question is whether Jalen Williams can return to being a legitimate second superstar. Biggest weakness: Reduced depth and the possibility of injuries affecting their rotation. Playoff prediction: Western Conference Finals. My verdict: They are the team I would least want to face in a seven-game series. If Williams is fully healthy, they could easily win the championship.
