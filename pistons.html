Pistons prediction

The Detroit Pistons are looking like one of the rising teams in the Eastern Conference for the 2026–27 NBA season. Led by Cade Cunningham, alongside Jalen Duren and their talented young supporting cast, the Pistons have the potential to become serious playoff contenders. Their biggest strengths are their athleticism, rebounding, defensive pressure, and Cunningham's ability to lead the offense. However, inconsistent shooting and limited playoff experience could create challenges against stronger teams. I predict Detroit will finish with approximately 48–52 wins, secure a top-four seed in the East, and reach at least the Eastern Conference Semifinals. If Cunningham continues playing at an All-Star level and Duren dominates inside, the Pistons could surprise everyone and make a deep playoff run.
