Raptors prediction

The Toronto Raptors are looking to have an exciting 2026–27 NBA season as they continue developing into a competitive team in the Eastern Conference. Led by Scottie Barnes, along with Brandon Ingram and RJ Barrett, the Raptors have a talented roster with strong scoring, athleticism, and defensive potential. Their biggest strengths are their versatility, young talent, and ability to push the pace offensively. However, consistency, outside shooting, and defensive execution could be challenges throughout the season. I predict Toronto will finish with approximately 40–45 wins, compete for a playoff spot, and potentially reach the first round of the playoffs. If Barnes continues improving and the team builds stronger chemistry, the Raptors could surprise several teams and become a dangerous opponent in the Eastern Conference.
