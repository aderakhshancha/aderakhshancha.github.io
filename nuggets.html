Nuggets prediction

The Denver Nuggets are expected to have another very competitive season in the 2026–27 NBA season, with a strong chance of becoming one of the top teams in the Western Conference and making a deep playoff run. Their biggest advantage is having Nikola Jokić, one of the best basketball players in the world, leading the team with his incredible scoring, rebounding, basketball IQ, and playmaking abilities. Alongside him, Jamal Murray will be extremely important because of his ability to score from the perimeter, create his own shots, and perform under pressure, especially during close games and the playoffs. Aaron Gordon will also play a major role by providing athleticism, defense, rebounding, and energy, while Christian Braun and the team's newer additions, including DeMar DeRozan and Marvin Bagley III, should help improve the team's depth and give Denver more offensive options. One of the biggest strengths of the Nuggets will likely be their offensive chemistry, since Jokić is an incredible passer who makes everyone around him better and allows the team to create high-quality scoring opportunities. However, Denver does have some weaknesses that could affect its performance throughout the season, particularly its defensive consistency, bench production, and ability to stay healthy. The team has also experienced some early-season struggles, starting with a 3–3 record through its first six games, showing that it still needs time to develop chemistry and find consistency. According to the NBA's season preview, improving defensively will be one of Denver's biggest priorities after ranking near the bottom of the league in defense last season. Overall, I believe the Nuggets have the talent and experience to win around 50–55 games, finish near the top of the Western Conference standings, and potentially make a run to the Western Conference Finals or even the NBA Finals. If Jokić continues playing at an MVP level, Murray stays healthy, Gordon remains an important defensive presence, and the new additions become comfortable in their roles, Denver could be one of the most dangerous teams in the entire NBA this season.
