Knicks prediction

The New York Knicks are looking like strong contenders in the Eastern Conference for the 2026–27 NBA season. Led by Jalen Brunson, alongside Karl-Anthony Towns, Mikal Bridges, OG Anunoby, and Josh Hart, the Knicks have the talent, defense, and experience to compete with the best teams in the league. Their biggest strengths are their physical play, strong defense, rebounding, and ability to perform in close games. Although they may struggle with consistency at times, I predict they will finish with 50–54 wins, secure a top-four seed in the East, and reach at least the Eastern Conference Finals. If Brunson continues playing at an elite level and the team stays healthy, the Knicks have a legitimate chance to reach the NBA Finals and compete for a championship.
