Hornets prediction

The Charlotte Hornets are looking to have a developing 2026–27 NBA season as they continue building a competitive young team in the Eastern Conference. Led by LaMelo Ball, the Hornets have exciting offensive potential, strong playmaking, and the ability to score quickly. Their biggest strengths are their speed, outside shooting, and young talent. However, injuries, defensive struggles, and inconsistency could make it difficult to compete with the East's top teams. I predict Charlotte will finish with approximately 28–33 wins and miss the playoffs. If LaMelo stays healthy and the young players continue improving, the Hornets could surprise some opponents and become a stronger playoff contender in the future.
