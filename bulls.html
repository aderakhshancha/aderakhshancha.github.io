Bulls prediction

The Chicago Bulls are looking to have a challenging but competitive 2026–27 NBA season as they try to establish themselves in the Eastern Conference. Led by their young talent and developing roster, the Bulls have the potential to improve through strong scoring, athleticism, and teamwork. Their biggest strengths are their offensive potential, energy, and ability to compete against tougher opponents. However, inconsistent defense, turnovers, and a lack of experience could make it difficult for them to compete with the East's top teams. I predict Chicago will finish with approximately 30–35 wins and miss the playoffs. If their young players continue developing and the team improves its consistency, the Bulls could surprise some opponents and build momentum for the future.
