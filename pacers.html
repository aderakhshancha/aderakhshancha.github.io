Pacers prediction

The Indiana Pacers are looking to have a challenging 2026–27 NBA season as they work to remain competitive in the Eastern Conference. With Tyrese Haliburton leading the team, the Pacers have the offensive talent and playmaking ability to compete against strong opponents. Their biggest strengths are their fast-paced offense, ball movement, and outside shooting. However, injuries, defensive consistency, and a lack of depth could make it difficult for Indiana to compete with the East's top teams. I predict the Pacers will finish with approximately 25–30 wins and miss the playoffs. If Haliburton returns to top form and the younger players step up, Indiana could surprise some teams and finish the season stronger than expected.
